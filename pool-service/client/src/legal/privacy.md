# Privacy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This page explains what Great Pool Friend keeps, why, and who can see it. In short: your business's data is yours, it is kept only to run the app for you, and it is never sold.

## What the app keeps

- **What your business enters:** customers and their contact details, pools and equipment, visits, water tests, chemicals, issues, notes, photos, invoices, payments and money records.
- **Logins:** each person's name, email and role. Passwords are stored only in a scrambled form (a "hash") that can't be turned back into the password.
- **Signed-in devices:** a short device name (for example "Chrome on Android") and when it was last used, so you can sign out a lost phone.
- **Feedback and error reports:** what someone writes in Send feedback, and errors the app runs into, with the screen, the app version and the language.
- **Server logs:** technical records of requests (including IP addresses), kept for a short time to keep the service running and secure.

The app does **not** use advertising, tracking or analytics services, and does not sell or share data.

## Cookies

One cookie keeps you logged in. Nothing else is stored in your browser except the app itself and your own data, so it can work without signal.

## Who can see it

- **Your business:** owners see everything; techs see their own route without any money figures.
- **Us (the operator):** only to run the service: backups, updates, security, and support when you ask. We don't look through your business's records otherwise.
- **Nobody else**, unless the law requires it.

## Where it is kept

- On servers we run, with each business in its own separate database. Connections to the app are encrypted (HTTPS, through Cloudflare).
- Nightly backups, plus an encrypted copy off the server (Cloudflare R2). Each business's backups have their own key.

## Emails

Your login email is used only for the app: password reset links, and notices to the business owner (for example, when someone on the team asks for a new password). No newsletters or marketing.

## How long

- While your business uses the app, its data is kept. Backups go back up to 12 months, so something deleted in the app can still be in an older backup for up to a year.
- When a business closes its account, a final backup is kept for 90 days, then everything is deleted, including the off-site copies.
- The owner can download everything at any time: More → Export everything.
