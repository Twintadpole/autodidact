# Great Pool Friend: beta tester guide

Thank you for testing Great Pool Friend! This guide explains what the beta is, what to try, and how to tell us what you find.

## What "beta" means

The app works and your data is safe, but it is still being polished. Things may change from week to week, and some parts are not finished yet (see "Not there yet" below). Your feedback decides what we fix and build next.

## What to try

**Everyone**

1. Install the app on your phone (Help → Getting started).
2. Work a normal week: the Route, visits with the timer, readings, tasks and chemicals, issues with photos.
3. Turn on airplane mode for a visit or two, then turn it off and check that everything uploads (the chip at the top says **Synced**).
4. Try the app in your own language (More → My account → Language).

**Owners**

1. Follow the Getting started card: business details, logo, chemical prices, customers, monthly rates.
2. Add a tech (More → Team), send the invite, and put pools on their route.
3. Make the month's invoices, share one as a PDF, and record a payment.
4. Look at Money, and at the Desktop view on a computer.

## How to send feedback

More → **Send feedback** (or the button at the bottom of any Help page). Pick Problem, Idea, Question or Other, and say:

- what you were doing,
- what you expected,
- what happened instead.

The screen you came from, the app version and your language are sent with it. If you have no signal, it is sent when you are back online. Errors the app runs into are reported to your business's own server automatically, so we often know about a problem before you tell us.

## Your data

- Each business has its own server space and its own database, kept apart from every other business.
- It is backed up every night, with an encrypted copy kept off the server.
- Your data is yours: it is never sold or shared.
- The owner can download everything at any time: More → Export everything (one Excel file).
- If a business leaves, a final backup is kept for 90 days, then deleted.

## Not there yet

- Invoices and visit recaps are in English only (the customer's own language comes later).
- Translations other than English are new: tell us about words that look wrong.
- Forgotten passwords: for now, the owner gives the person a new temporary password (More → Team). Reset by email comes later.
