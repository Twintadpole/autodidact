## Your first 15 minutes

1. On your phone, open the address in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone) and log in.
2. Choose your own password and your language.
3. Install the app. Android: tap the ⋮ menu, then **Install app**. iPhone: tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Follow the **Getting started** card at the top of the Route: business details and logo, chemical prices, customers and pools, monthly rates, techs.
5. Add your techs: **More → Team → Add a login**, then tap **Invite** to send them their login.

## Good to know

- **No signal?** Keep working. Everything is saved on the phone and uploads by itself.
- **On a computer:** More → Desktop view gives you tables and a dashboard.
- **Help:** tap **?** at the top of any screen. **Feedback:** More → Send feedback.
- **Your data is yours.** It is backed up every night, with an encrypted copy kept off the server. You can download all of it at any time (More → Export everything). It is never sold or shared.
